### B. Critical / high functional bugs
6. **Cookie consent banner has no background (transparent) → illegible, overlaps content on every page/locale** [design C1 + clutter H4 + qa adjacency · CRITICAL · Code]. Root cause verified: `bg-bg-raised` is an undefined token. **Fix: change `bg-bg-raised` → `bg-bg-surface`** in `src/components/analytics/CookieNotice.tsx`, confirm opaque with top border + shadow, and ensure it reserves space above the mobile tab bar (fixes design M5/M6 overlap + the clipped FR "En savoir plus"). *This is a regression I introduced in the consent task.* **Bonus:** the other 5 components using `bg-bg-raised` are also transparent — worth a separate sweep.
7. **Unmatched URLs render the unbranded default 404** [design C2 + clutter C3 + qa HIGH-1 · CRITICAL · Code]. Status is 404 (good) but the page is Next's built-in (English, no lang/dir, no chrome). The localized `src/app/[locale]/not-found.tsx` only fires for `notFound()` inside matched routes. Fix: add a branded root `src/app/not-found.tsx` and/or a catch-all `src/app/[locale]/[...rest]/page.tsx` that calls `notFound()`, so typo/old/bot URLs get the localized, branded page. (This makes the earlier I18N-03 fix complete.)
8. **Soft-404: missing player/team/match return HTTP 200** [qa HIGH-2 · HIGH · Code/SEO · NEEDS-HUMAN verify]. Pages call `notFound()` (joueur:99, equipe:172, match:198/204) and render the 404 body, but status is 200 and title is the generic fallback — Google will flag "Soft 404" across 45k sitemap URLs. Likely a PPR/cacheComponents streaming interaction. **Re-test on a production build** (`pnpm build && pnpm start`); if 200 persists, this is the top SEO blocker. Fix: ensure `notFound()` yields a 404 status (call before the streamed boundary) + localized 404 title.
9. **No way to withdraw cookie consent** [qa HIGH-3 · HIGH · Code/Legal]. The banner is the only `setConsent` site and never reappears after a choice; no "Cookie settings" control. GDPR Art. 7(3) requires withdrawal to be as easy as giving. Fix: a persistent "Cookie settings" link (footer and/or `/cookies`) that reopens the chooser and tears down GTM/pixel on withdrawal.
10. **Player "club" shows the national team ("Morocco")** [clutter H2 · HIGH · Data/NEEDS-HUMAN]. Header + Player Info headline the national team, not the current club (career shows → PSG). Map "club" to the current club side. `src/components/player/`.
11. **"—" placeholders where scorer names / stats belong** [clutter H3 + design M1 · HIGH · Data]. Match recap/events/FAQ ("Madagascar: — (13'), — (53')") and player season table ("Apps —, Rating —"). Suppress empty rows/fields or backfill, rather than showing dashes.

### D. Medium / low (functional, security, a11y)

21. **Skip-link not localized** [qa MED-4 · LOW]. Hardcoded English "Skip to content" on fr/ar. `src/app/[locale]/(app)/layout.tsx:27`.
22. **Sitemap emits raw non-ASCII Arabic in `<loc>`** [qa LOW-2 · LOW]. Percent-encode (Google tolerates it). `src/app/sitemap.ts`.
23. **CookieNotice `role="dialog"` lacks focus management / Esc; verbose aria-label** [qa LOW-3 · LOW].
24. **PlayerPhotoCircle `alt=""`** [qa LOW-4 · LOW]. Verify it's never the sole identifier in stats tables.
