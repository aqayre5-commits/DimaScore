## 13. KEY OBSERVATIONS & UNIQUE FEATURES OF THE PLAYER PAGE

The player page has several elements not found on team or tournament pages:

1. PLAYER VALUE CARD — Market value display with crowd-sourced "higher or lower?" voting (€ up/down buttons). This is unique to player pages.

2. SUMMARY CHART — Monthly average rating bar chart (last 12 months) with toggleable values. Visual performance summary not found on team pages.

3. ATTRIBUTE OVERVIEW — Pentagon radar chart showing 5 composite attribute scores (ATT/TEC/TAC/DEF/CRE) with a time-scrubbing slider going back 3+ years. Unique to players.

4. PLAYER POSITIONS — Mini football pitch with positional dots plus Strengths/Weaknesses text labels. Unique to player pages.

5. TRANSFER HISTORY — Line chart + chronological list of all club moves with fees. Unique to player pages.

6. NATIONAL TEAM CARD — Shows international career summary (debut, appearances, goals) with link to national team page. Unique to player pages.

7. CAREER TAB — Full season-by-season statistical table (all clubs + competitions) from 2015-2026, expandable per season to show per-competition breakdown. Unique to player pages.

8. FANTASY TAB — Shows fantasy points per match, price, selection percentage, and per-round breakdown. Competition-selectable. Unique to player pages.

9. STICKY HEADER — Unlike team pages which show a compact match widget in the sticky header, the player page shows a simpler sticky bar with just the player name, photo, and favourite toggle.

10. BREADCRUMB CONTEXT — Breadcrumb reflects the player's primary competition context: Football > [Country] > [Primary Competition] > [Player name]. For Bernardo Silva this shows Football > England > Premier League.

11. NO TABS ON LEFT COLUMN — Unlike team pages which have multiple switchable views in the left column (List/Calendar, Finished/Upcoming), the player page left column is entirely static cards with no tabbed interaction (except the competition/season dropdowns within the right-column tabs).

12. PREVIOUS/NEXT MATCH in profile header — Both shown as clickable cards with competition context. Team pages only show an inline match widget.
